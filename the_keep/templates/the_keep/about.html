{% trans 'About the Database' %}

The {{site_title}} is an unofficial collection of fan factions and components for Root, the board game by Leder Games.

The {{site_title}} started in 2024 as a spreadsheet listing popular fan factions. The goal was to provide an easy way to search Fan Content made by the Root community with links to discussion boards and Discord threads. After countless hours of tutorials and research the Database made the jump to the web and was made public in mid 2025. Many features have been added since, with more to come. You can see the most recent update here.

{% trans "Community Integrations" %}

Root Digital League

Root Digital League is organized by the Woodland Warriors Discord community and are an informal and fun way to play against real players. These games are recorded on the Root Digital League website and imported to the {{site_title}} through the Match Viewset List API provided by Pliskin.

Leder Rules Library

The official and up to date rules for Root can be found at the Leder Rules Library. The {{site_title}} updates its Law of Root to match the publicly available rules provided by the Leder Cards repository on Github{% if law_date %} and was last updated on {{law_date|date:"F j, Y"}}.{% else %}.{% endif %}

RootELO

The {{site_title}} uses RootELO to display ELO rankings for Root Digital League and HOOT. RootELO was developed by Tricholome as an alternate to a classic winrate leaderboard. Results dynamically increase or decrease a players ranking based on the opponents they played against.
